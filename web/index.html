SESNSeafarers Emergency Support Network

Distress coordination,
from alert to approved action.

A vessel goes quiet, runs aground, or calls for help. SESN classifies what happened, names the rescue authority legally responsible for that water, finds the nearest hull that can actually reach them, and drafts a different message for every party who needs one, then stops and waits for a human to approve each one.
